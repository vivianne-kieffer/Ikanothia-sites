The Artist's Journey

Gilbert Kieffer is a multi-talented French philosopher and painter, born in Strasbourg, France in 1953. Throughout his life, he has lived in countries around the world, exploring different cultures and artistic techniques that fuel his creative genius.

In 1988, he created an ambitious exhibition installation called "The Apocalypse" at Reunion Airport—a polyptych expression of 21 paintings adorning a three-ton support structure. His work has been recognized by the French government with two Palms awards from the Academies in 1999 and 2006, distinctions famously created by Napoleon Bonaparte.

Gilbert's practice incorporates petroglyphic representations and digital art, blending philosophical theories with contemporary visual language. His accomplishments demonstrate a commitment to exploring the delicate balance between technical mastery and profound spiritual resonance.

Convinced of the importance of this balance, Gilbert asserts that acknowledging and embracing this vision will shape the essence of art in both the present and future. He is represented by Elity Art Gallery in the United States.
