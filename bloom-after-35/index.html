The Founder

Vivianne Kieffer

Founder & ICF ACC Coach

Bloom After 35 began with a question I could not put down: why do so many capable women arrive at their most powerful decade and find the room has been arranged for someone else?

I coach women through the two transitions that most often collide in this chapter — the professional one and the personal one. Not to choose between them, but to hold both without losing yourself in either.

  • ICF ACC — Associate Certified Coach, International Coaching Federation
  • Executive coaching certification
  • Founder of Bloom After 35 and Ikanothia
